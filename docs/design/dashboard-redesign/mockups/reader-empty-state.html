Start here

There's a manuscript out there waiting for the right reader.

Authors on Bookending share their works-in-progress with a small circle of readers before publication. You read drafts, leave notes in the margin, and write directly to the author. The book gets better. So does your reading.

§ How reading works here

Discover, read, connect.

§ 01 Discover

Find a manuscript that wants you in the room.

Browse open beta reads, apply where authors are accepting readers, or set up a public profile so authors can invite you to their work.

§ 02 Read

Read in the margin. Notes are part of the work.

Inline notes, replies, passage shares. Read at your own pace — there are no due dates, just the conversation while it's warm.

§ 03 Connect

Write to the author. Stay for the next book.

When you finish, send a Letter through Correspondence. Authors hear directly from the readers who shaped the book.

§ Three ways authors invite readers

Authors choose who joins each beta read.

OPEN APPLY

Anyone can apply. Authors review applications and accept readers who fit.

PROFILE MATCH

Authors browse public reader profiles and send invitations directly.

CIRCLE

When readers in your circle join a beta, you'll see it in your Library.

§ Books, too
